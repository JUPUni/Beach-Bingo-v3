02What’s in it

One adventure. Five rooms. Your friends.

Play the islands on your own, practise in a hall full of labelled bots, or open a live room and play the same balls as your friends.

  • Adventure

    40 island levels

    Palm Cove, Coral Reef, Shipwreck Bay and Volcano Isle, ten boards each. Call bingo in fewer balls for up to three stars.

  • Boosters

    Four boosters

    Seagull daubs every call for you. Crab pinches any square. Big Wave adds five balls. Golden Sun doubles your coins.

  • 75-ball

    Sunset Hall

    A line, two lines, then blackout, with a jackpot for a blackout inside 48 balls. Up to four cards a game.

  • 90-ball

    Pier Hall

    Classic 90-ball: one line, two lines, full house. Up to three cards a game.

  • Speed · royale · duel

    Three more rooms

    Wave Rush is 30-ball speed bingo. Last Castle Standing washes out the bottom half every five balls. Riptide Duel is one on one, with no auto-daub.

  • Live rooms

    Play with friends

    Open Sunset Hall, Pier Hall, Wave Rush or Riptide Duel as a live room, share a five-letter code, and every phone sees the same balls and checks every win. No server, no sign-up.

  • Free to play

    Coins are play money

    Coins are free and have no cash value. Run dry, and 500 more wash up every four hours.

03Fair play

Check every round yourself

Every draw comes from seeds the game commits to before you play, so no ball can be changed after the fact.

  1. 1

    Commit

    Before the first ball, the game shows you the SHA-256 hash of its secret server seed.

  2. 2

    Mix

    Your own client seed and the round number go into every draw, through HMAC-SHA256.

  3. 3

    Reveal

    Rotate the seeds and the old server seed is revealed. Hash it, match it, and replay every draw it made.
