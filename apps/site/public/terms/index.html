TermsHow the game is played

Free to play. Shells are free; coins stay in the game.

These are the terms for playing Beach Bingo in a browser or in the Android app. They are short because the game is simple: shells are free, coins are bought and never cashed out, and nothing of value is ever paid out.

1. Who we are

Beach Bingo is a Fete Labs game, managed under Saltwater Brands. In these terms, "we" and "us" mean the team that makes and runs it, and "the game" means the game at beachbingo.xyz/app/, the browser rooms at /play, and the Beach Bingo app for Android. By playing, you agree to these terms.

2. Coins and shells

Shells are the game's free play money. The game gives them to you, more wash up on a timer when you run dry, and they are never bought or sold. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred, exchanged for anything of value or withdrawn.

Coins are bought in the Coin Shop, in packs paid with SOL, USDC, PYUSD, JUP or SKR, or granted by a promotion such as the Free Game ticket. A purchase is final: coins have no cash value, cannot be sold, transferred between players, exchanged for anything of value, refunded or withdrawn, and never leave the game. A prize won on a coin table is paid in coins and nothing else. We may change a pack's price or contents at any time; coins already bought are not affected.

Every mode plays with either currency at the same prices and the same prizes; the switch in the top bar picks which. Cards in the bingo halls, stakes in Casino Cove and prize pools are counted in shells or in coins and nothing else. Nothing of value is ever paid out.

3. Fair play

Every draw comes from seeds the game commits to before you play, as described on the landing page and in the game under Settings → Provably fair. You may check any round yourself, and we encourage it. In a live room, the host's browser commits to the seed before anyone buys a card and every player's browser verifies the reveal, the cards and every win independently; a client that has been tampered with cannot change what the other players see.

Play the game as it is offered. Do not use automation, exploits or modified clients to disrupt other players' rooms, and do not use the live rooms or the player name to harass anyone. Live rooms are meant for people who know each other: anyone with a room code can join a room while it is open, so share codes with the people you want to play with.

4. Live rooms

Live rooms are played directly between the players' browsers, with public relays run by third parties used only to find each other. We operate no game server, hold no record of a room, and cannot restore a round that was interrupted by a lost connection, a closed tab or a relay that stopped answering. Coins spent on cards for a round that never started are returned by your own game; cards in a round that did start belong to that round.

5. Age, and where coins are offered

Beach Bingo asks for no personal details, and play with shells is free and open to everyone. The coin tables and the Coin Shop are for players aged 18 and over: before they open, you confirm once that you are 18 or older and not a resident of Washington State, and the game checks the region of your connection (see the privacy notice). Coins are not offered to players in Washington State. The game includes a play reminder, a daily loss limit, a daily cap on coins bought and a cool-off under Settings → Responsible play.

6. The game as it is

Beach Bingo is provided as it is and as available, without warranties of any kind, to the fullest extent the law allows. We may change, pause or stop the game, its modes, its rewards or these terms at any time; the date at the top of this page moves when the terms change. Because neither shells nor coins have cash value, a change to the game never takes anything of value from you.
