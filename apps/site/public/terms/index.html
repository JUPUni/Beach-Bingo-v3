TermsHow the game is played

Free to play. Coins are play money.

These are the terms for playing Beach Bingo in a browser or in the Android app. They are short because the game is simple: nothing is bought, nothing is staked, nothing is owed.

1. Who we are

Beach Bingo is a Fete Labs game, managed under Saltwater Brands. In these terms, "we" and "us" mean the team that makes and runs it, and "the game" means the game at beachbingo.xyz/app/, the browser rooms at /play, and the Beach Bingo app for Android. By playing, you agree to these terms.

2. Coins are play money

Coins are the game's play money. They are free: the game gives them to you, and more wash up on a timer when you run dry. They have no cash value. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred between players, exchanged for anything of value or withdrawn, and we will never ask you to pay for them. Cards in the bingo halls, stakes in Casino Cove and prize pools are all counted in coins and nothing else. Nothing of value is staked or won in Beach Bingo, and it is not gambling.

3. Fair play

Every draw comes from seeds the game commits to before you play, as described on the landing page and in the game under Settings → Provably fair. You may check any round yourself, and we encourage it. In a live room, the host's browser commits to the seed before anyone buys a card and every player's browser verifies the reveal, the cards and every win independently; a client that has been tampered with cannot change what the other players see.

Play the game as it is offered. Do not use automation, exploits or modified clients to disrupt other players' rooms, and do not use the live rooms or the player name to harass anyone. Live rooms are meant for people who know each other: anyone with a room code can join a room while it is open, so share codes with the people you want to play with.

4. Live rooms

Live rooms are played directly between the players' browsers, with public relays run by third parties used only to find each other. We operate no game server, hold no record of a room, and cannot restore a round that was interrupted by a lost connection, a closed tab or a relay that stopped answering. Coins spent on cards for a round that never started are returned by your own game; cards in a round that did start belong to that round.

5. Age

Beach Bingo asks for no personal details and is free to play. Casino Cove's casino-style games are recommended for players aged 18 and over, and the game includes a play reminder and optional limits under Settings → Responsible play.

6. The game as it is

Beach Bingo is provided as it is and as available, without warranties of any kind, to the fullest extent the law allows. We may change, pause or stop the game, its modes, its rewards or these terms at any time; the date at the top of this page moves when the terms change. Because coins have no value, a change to the game never takes anything of value from you.
